## Risk priorities

1. Stock marker prevents a normal second deduction, but SQLite row-lock expectations, duplicate payments in `pay_order`, and edits to already-deducted unpaid bills prevent a blanket exactly-once guarantee.
2. FIFO layers and product totals diverge on restore/refund/correction; cart-time price/cost allocation is not a persisted final consumption allocation. Unit conversion must wait for a reconciliation strategy.
3. Installments would duplicate revenue and misdate collections under current reports; supplier payment as Expense could also double-charge cost/profit. Approve reporting definitions before release.
4. Historical payments/documents can be rewritten/deleted; defaults/backfills must not label every unpaid bill a supplied invoice or invent historical supplier/unit facts.
5. CR-07's stock-on-order and sales-reduction language conflicts with accurate physical inventory and separate settlement reporting; explicitly discuss before implementation.
